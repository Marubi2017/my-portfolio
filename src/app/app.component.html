About Me

I’m Michelle, a full-stack developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, specializing in building accessible, user-friendly web applications that turn complex ideas into intuitive experiences.

Growing up in Nigeria, I was always curious about technology and how things come together behind the scenes. I’ve since channeled that curiosity into my career as a software developer. I started by building a strong foundation in backend development, working with databases and server-side logic, before expanding into frontend development with tools like React, TypeScript, and modern web frameworks. Today, I focus on building seamless, accessible experiences that transform complex ideas into products people can easily understand and enjoy.
